Every future recommendation must assess company fundamentals, valuation, earnings dates and results, material news, current price and liquidity, downside risk, and portfolio concentration. It must show an entry range, target, invalidation/exit level, expected holding period, rationale, source timestamps, and what could go wrong.

Decision rules: BUY only when evidence and risk/reward support it; WAIT when price or an imminent event makes the setup unattractive; AVOID when valuation, business risk, or data quality fails the checks. Missing or stale data means no actionable recommendation.

Scoring method

Revenue growth: 25 points. Net profit margin: 15 points. Approximate free-cash-flow margin: 20 points. One-year price return: 20 points. Maximum drawdown: 20 points. A candidate is excluded if any component is missing or ambiguous. Scores rank research priorities only; valuation, event risk, and walk-forward validation are not yet part of this score.
