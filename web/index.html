    Provisional signals · not validated

    The experimental watchlist score ranks research priorities only. Preliminary valuation-based watch zones may appear in the analysis panel when inputs pass quality checks, but they are provisional research signals only; full historical validation remains incomplete.

    The system compares 60 selected US stocks and displays up to five qualified research candidates and, where the latest quote and valuation inputs pass checks, shows a model entry watch zone, fair-value reference, risk reference, and a 1–6 month review horizon. These are not guaranteed buy/exit instructions; the strategy has not passed walk-forward and untouched holdout validation.

    Not run

    Tap “Suggest me stocks” to screen a cross-sector starter universe of 60 US stocks. The starter universe is not the full market; results depend on provider availability and strict data-quality checks.

    Scoring method

    Revenue growth: 25 points. Net profit margin: 15 points. Approximate free-cash-flow margin: 20 points. One-year price return: 20 points. Maximum drawdown: 20 points. A candidate is excluded if any component is missing or ambiguous. Scores rank research priorities only; valuation, event risk, and walk-forward validation are not yet part of this score.
